Privacy Policy

SCR Feed Manager app (SCR Feed & SCR Stars Club) · Developer: Omega UI, LLC

This privacy policy applies to the SCR Feed Manager Android app (also called SCR Feed and SCR Stars Club, "the app"), published on Google Play by the developer Omega UI, LLC. Omega UI, LLC runs the app for Sweet Cypress Ranch, a feed and supply store in Fort Myers and LaBelle, Florida. It explains what the app collects, why, and your choices.

Last updated: November 26, 2026

What we collect

  • Account details: name, email address, phone number and a user ID.
  • Delivery address, so we can deliver your orders.
  • Purchase history: what you ordered and when.
  • Content you add (optional): photos, notes such as vet information, and messages sent through the app.
  • Device ID for app notifications, such as fresh hay arrivals.
  • App usage and crash reports, to fix problems and improve the app.

We do not collect your precise location, contacts or health information. Card payments are handled by our payment processors (Shopify / Stripe); the app does not store your card number.

How we use it

  • To run your account, take and deliver orders, and show your order history.
  • To track SCR Stars points and membership.
  • To send you store updates and notifications you can turn off.
  • To keep the app working and fix bugs.

We do not use your data for third-party advertising.

Sharing

We do not sell your data. We use trusted service providers to run the app (such as hosting, database, store and messaging services). They process data only on our behalf and under our instructions. We may disclose data if the law requires it.

Security

All data sent between the app and our servers is encrypted in transit.

Keeping and deleting your data

We keep your data while your account is open. You can ask us to delete your account or specific data at any time — see Delete Your Account. Sales records are kept as long as the law requires for tax and accounting.

Children

The app is meant for adults and is not directed to children under 13.
