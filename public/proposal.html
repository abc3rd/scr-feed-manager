FAQ

No vague promises.

Does SynCloud replace QuickBooks?

No. QuickBooks remains the accounting system. Shopify online transactions can be imported through the Shopify Connector; in-store sales remain in the existing register/bookkeeping routine.

Does an online order immediately reduce inventory?

Not in the documented workflow. Final inventory deduction is tied to the yard/warehouse crew tapping Done after loading.

Are projected returns guaranteed?

No. The proposal calls them illustrative projections dependent on assumptions.

Is SEO guaranteed?

No. The proposal says results can take 3–6 months and gives no ranking or traffic guarantee.

Is the QR code on this page live?

No. Production QR codes should point to the actual reorder URL and be tested before printing.
