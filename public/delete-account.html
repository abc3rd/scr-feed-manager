SCR Feed & SCR Stars Club app · Sweet Cypress Ranch

How to request deletion

  1. Tap the button below (or email starsscr@gmail.com).
  2. Use the subject "Delete my SCR account".
  3. Include the email address or phone number on your account so we can find it.
  4. We will confirm by reply, then delete your account within 30 days.
Request account deletion

You do not need the app installed to make this request.

What we delete

  • Your account and login
  • Profile details: name, email, phone number and delivery address
  • Photos, notes and other content you added
  • Messages sent through the app
  • SCR Stars points and membership details
  • Device IDs used for app notifications

What we keep, and for how long

  • Sales and invoice records (what was bought, when and for how much) are kept as long as the law requires for tax and accounting purposes, then deleted.
  • These records are not used for marketing after your account is deleted.

Delete some data without closing your account

Email starsscr@gmail.com and tell us what to remove (for example, photos or your delivery address). We will remove it within 30 days and keep your account open.
